A tiny guard against prompt injection.

Prompt injection is text written to hijack an AI, like "ignore your instructions and…", typed by a user or hidden in an email or web page. This small Transformer reads the text first and flags the attack before it reaches the model. It is light enough to run on your own device, for free.

  • 14× smaller than leading open guards
  • about 4× faster
  • your text stays on your device
  • free to run
