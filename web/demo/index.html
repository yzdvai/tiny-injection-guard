Harmless prompts wrongly blocked

lower is better

339 harmless prompts full of words like "ignore", "bypass" or "override" (the NotInject benchmark).

Small and private

14× fewer parameters and a 52× smaller download than the leading open guards. It runs on your own device: no server, no hosting bill, and your text never leaves your browser.

Rarely in your way

It wrongly blocks about 2% of tricky harmless prompts, where the big guards block 12% and 43%. A guard that cries wolf gets switched off.

A first line of defence

It's tuned to be cautious. It rarely blocks harmless text, but it can miss attacks written in unfamiliar styles, which is why it's meant as a first layer, not the only one.

Recall: the share of real attacks the model flags. False-positive rate: the share of harmless texts it flags. "Same sources" means held-out texts from the six datasets used for training; the other sets come from elsewhere. Thresholds: ours 0.945 (tuned for at most 1% false alarms on validation data), the open guards 0.5 (their default). PIGuard was trained on deepset, and ProtectAI on two of our training sources, so those scores are not fully held out for them. We chose our model after seeing two training runs' test results, so its numbers are slightly optimistic. Its ONNX int8 version gives the same verdict as the full model on 99.6% of the test texts.

How it works

Three steps, each designed to avoid rewarding a model for memorising dataset style.

Explore the architecture, layer by layer →
  1. 1

    Data

    About 20k texts from six public datasets. Exact and near-duplicates removed, related texts kept on the same side of the split, and whole sources held out for testing.

  2. 2

    Fine-tune

    ELECTRA-small (14M parameters) trained as benign vs injection with early stopping. The decision threshold is tuned so at most 1% of harmless validation texts are flagged.

  3. 3

    Ship

    Exported to ONNX and quantized to int8 (54 → 14 MB). It runs in your browser with transformers.js: no server, and no LLM is called. The demo only runs the guard.
