Good at

Rarely blocks harmless text: about 2% false alarms on NotInject, against 12% for PIGuard and 43% for ProtectAI, at 1/14 of their size.

Misses

Most attacks written in styles it never saw, especially instructions hidden inside emails or documents. A simple TF-IDF baseline scores about as well on the held-out sets. English only.

Honest caveats

We picked this model after seeing two training runs' test results, so its numbers are slightly optimistic. The big guards were trained on 15–20 datasets, including attack styles we did not have.

How it works

Three steps, each designed to avoid rewarding a model for memorising dataset style.

  1. 1

    Data

    About 20k texts from six public datasets. Exact and near-duplicates removed, related texts kept on the same side of the split, and whole sources held out for testing.

  2. 2

    Fine-tune

    ELECTRA-small (14M parameters) trained as benign vs injection with early stopping. The decision threshold is tuned so at most 1% of harmless validation texts are flagged.

  3. 3

    Ship

    Quantized to int8 (54 → 26 MB) with no measurable accuracy cost, and served on a free 2-vCPU machine. This demo calls no LLM; it only runs the guard.
